# Global News To Content Pipeline - Research Report

## Purpose

This report is a Notion-ready research page for the automation demo. The workflow should take a broad topic, research current signals, identify stories with social potential, and turn them into content opportunities.

## Research Topic

Current global news stories with strong social content potential.

## Selection Criteria

- Important enough for a general audience to care about.
- Connected to technology, business, AI, society, culture, or future trends.
- Easy to explain in one short post or carousel.
- Likely to create curiosity, discussion, or useful reflection.
- Supported by credible sources and visible social conversation.

## Research Workflow

1. Use general research to understand what happened and why it matters.
2. Use X/social research to understand how people are reacting.
3. Pick 3-5 stories with the strongest content potential.
4. Convert each story into a clear content angle.
5. Package the final captions and visual directions in a ready-to-post page.
